About

I am a Ph.D. candidate in the School of Electrical Engineering at KAIST, advised by Prof. Munchurl Kim in the Video and Image Computing Lab (VICLab). I received my M.S. in Electrical Engineering from KAIST in 2021 and my B.S. from the KAIST School of Computing in 2019.

My research focuses on deep learning for low-level vision toward video restoration, including video super-resolution and frame interpolation, arbitrary-scale restoration, and diffusion- and flow-matching-based generative priors for perceptually faithful reconstruction.

  • Video super-resolution
  • Video frame interpolation
  • Arbitrary-scale restoration
  • Diffusion & flow-matching priors
